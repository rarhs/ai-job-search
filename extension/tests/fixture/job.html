Senior Data Scientist

Windward Analytics · Copenhagen, Denmark · Full-time · Hybrid (2 days office)

About the role

We are looking for a Senior Data Scientist to join our forecasting team. You will design, train and deploy machine learning models that predict energy production for wind farms across Northern Europe, working closely with meteorologists and platform engineers.

What you will do

  • Build and maintain time-series forecasting models (gradient boosting, deep learning)
  • Own the full model lifecycle: data pipelines, training, evaluation, monitoring
  • Collaborate with domain experts to encode physical constraints into models
  • Present findings to commercial stakeholders and customers

What we expect

  • MSc or PhD in a quantitative field
  • 5+ years of Python for production machine learning (pandas, scikit-learn, PyTorch)
  • Experience with cloud ML infrastructure (we use AWS and Kubernetes)
  • Strong communication skills in English; Danish is a plus

Nice to have

  • Domain knowledge in energy, meteorology or geophysics
  • Experience with MLOps tooling and model monitoring

Questions? Contact Head of Data Science, Marie Holm. We review applications continuously.
